Privacy

Privacy information

A plain-language summary of how this site currently handles visitor information.

SaaS Fieldbook is currently a static informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not currently provide user accounts, comments, checkout or an on-site contact form.

Information you send us

If you contact SaaS Fieldbook by email, the information you include in that message is used to respond to your enquiry and maintain normal correspondence.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website. GitHub’s own privacy documentation governs its processing.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, pages viewed, article engagement and outbound link clicks. Google may process device and browser information, referral sources and approximate location derived from technical connection data. Analytics cookies identify a browser; we do not intentionally send names, email addresses or other directly identifying information to Analytics.

The Google Analytics tag loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. If you reject analytics, no Analytics tag is loaded. Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our tag configuration. Our Analytics cookies are configured for a 180-day lifetime, which can renew on subsequent visits.

We save your analytics choice in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. You can change or withdraw it using “Analytics preferences” in the footer of any page. Withdrawal stops future tracking and removes this site’s Analytics cookies; it does not automatically delete data already collected. If you clear your browser storage, we will ask again.

Google processes Analytics data under its applicable service terms and privacy policies. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Contact us below with questions about your information.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain clearly disclosed affiliate links. A third-party merchant or affiliate platform may use its own tracking technology after you follow such a link. Their privacy policies apply to that processing.
