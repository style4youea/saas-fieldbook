Disclosure

Affiliate and commercial disclosure

How SaaS Fieldbook handles commissions, sponsorships and editorial independence.

Some SaaS Fieldbook pages may contain affiliate links. If you follow one and later become a paying customer, SaaS Fieldbook may receive a commission. When an affiliate relationship applies to a page, we aim to make that relationship clear.

Affiliate relationships do not equal endorsements

Participation in an affiliate program does not guarantee a positive review, a recommendation or inclusion in a comparison. We aim to judge products by their fit, trade-offs, cost and evidence available to us.

Research is labeled by evidence

A commercial relationship does not allow us to describe vendor documentation as hands-on experience. Pages based primarily on public vendor information are labeled as vendor research until meaningful testing has been completed.

Sponsored content

If SaaS Fieldbook accepts fixed payment for eligible content in the future, the page will identify the commercial relationship. Payment does not buy an undisclosed favorable opinion.

Your price

Affiliate commissions generally do not increase the price paid by the reader unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Offers, pricing and program terms can change, so the vendor’s checkout and terms control the final transaction.

Questions

Questions about a specific relationship can be sent through the contact page.
