About SaaS Fieldbook

Practical software research for small teams.

SaaS Fieldbook is an independent editorial project focused on software decisions where price, setup effort and day-to-day usefulness matter more than hype.

Editor

Evija Āboliņa

Evija is the founder and editor of SaaS Fieldbook. Her work focuses on practical software research for small businesses, ecommerce operations and written digital workflows. She combines firsthand platform use with primary-source research and clearly labels where hands-on testing has not yet been completed.

Why this site exists

Software pages are good at listing features. Buyers still have to answer harder questions: Does this solve our actual problem? How much work will implementation create? What happens when usage grows? What are the trade-offs? SaaS Fieldbook is built around those questions.

Our evidence labels

Hands-on review

Reserved for products we have actually used in a meaningful workflow. A hands-on label should explain what was tested and what was not.

Vendor research

Used when a page is based on public product documentation, pricing pages and other primary vendor information but hands-on testing is not complete. These pages can be useful, but they are not presented as personal product experience.

Editorial guide

General decision frameworks and operational guidance written independently of a specific vendor.

How we research

We prefer primary sources such as official pricing pages, product documentation and terms. When firsthand use is available, we distinguish observations from vendor claims. Material facts are checked near publication, and pages are updated when important pricing, product or program information changes.

How we judge a product

Our core criteria are problem fit, time-to-value, setup effort, reliability, total cost, operational risk, data portability and the quality of the exit route. A feature only matters when it improves a real workflow.

Corrections and updates

SaaS products change quickly. Pricing, limits and features can become outdated. Material corrections are welcome through the contact address, and time-sensitive pages include a checked or updated date where practical. We correct substantive errors rather than quietly preserving outdated claims.
